# Alice — BEP Proposal Writing Standards

## Document Structure (5 Sections — Official Template)
Every BEP proposal follows this exact structure:

1. **Initial Conversation** — 3-4 paragraphs introducing the partnership opportunity. Must include:
   - Opening: "British Enrolment Partners ('BEP') is pleased to present this proposal to [SCHOOL]..."
   - Recognition of the school's existing strengths and recruitment context
   - BEP capability paragraph (global network, in-region teams in China, SE Asia, Latin America)
   - School retains control bullet list (5 mandatory items)

2. **The Models: Fixed Retainer v Performance Partnership** — Always present BOTH models:
   - Fixed Retainer: £4,800/month + VAT, no enrolment-based charge
   - Performance Partnership: No upfront cost, 15% operational service fee per year of enrolment
   - Agent commission always separate from BEP fees

3. **What [School] Gains** — 7 bullet points based on standard baseline:
   - Stronger international pipeline
   - Robust partner network
   - Always-on recruitment function
   - Better conversion rates
   - Strategic market development
   - Better use of internal team
   - Greater visibility and control

4. **Recommendation and Next Steps** — Hardcoded from template:
   - Confirm preferred model
   - Agree targets & roadmap
   - BEP team training (in-person UK + online overseas)
   - Coordinated marketing plan

## Tone & Style Requirements
- Professional British English (no contractions)
- Persuasive but understated — never aggressive
- Never use "guarantee" or promise specific student numbers
- Never disclose BEP's internal financial targets
- Use "enrolment" (British spelling), not "enrollment"
- Always reference "pupil fit, nationality balance and long-term retention"

## Forbidden Content
- No specific student number commitments
- No promises about particular markets without prior agreement
- No mention of competitor agencies by name
- No modification of the £4,800/month or 15% fee figures without explicit approval
- No reference to "exclusive" partnerships unless contractually agreed
